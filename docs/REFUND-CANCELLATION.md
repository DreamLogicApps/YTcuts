# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Last updated: 2026-09-30**

YT Cuts has no paid plans, required checkout, subscription, license fee, or gated functionality. Core use remains free. The optional **Support the Project** link may direct a supporter to Buy Me a Coffee; it is not a purchase of access or a subscription.

YT Cuts does not process or store those payments. Questions about a contribution, receipt, cancellation, or refund should use Buy Me a Coffee's support process and the terms shown there. Applicable consumer rights may also apply depending on the supporter's location.

A user can request cancellation of an active local download using **Stop Download**. Cancellation attempts to terminate the local `yt-dlp` process and remove that task's temporary files. A process may take a short time to stop, and a completed file is not automatically deleted by cancellation.

If the project changes from voluntary contributions to paid functionality, this document must be replaced with product-specific pricing, renewal, cancellation, refund timing, statutory consumer rights, support contact details, and payment-provider terms before that change is released. It should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel and a tax professional for each target market.
